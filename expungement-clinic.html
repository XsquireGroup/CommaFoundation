Before You Register

Am I Eligible?

This clinic may be able to help if you have:

  • ✓ Misdemeanor convictions in Hamilton County, Ohio
  • ✓ Felony convictions in Hamilton County, Ohio
  • ✓ Arrests that did not result in conviction
  • ✓ Cases that were dismissed or acquitted
  • ✓ Juvenile records in Hamilton County
Not sure if you qualify? Come anyway. Our licensed attorneys will review your specific situation and advise you on all available options — including records that may not be eligible for expungement but could qualify for sealing.

This clinic cannot help with:

  • ✕ Charges from outside Hamilton County, Ohio
  • ✕ Federal criminal convictions
  • ✕ Out-of-state charges or convictions
  • ✕ Certain violent felonies — your attorney will advise
  • ✕ Sex offense convictions requiring registration
Day-of Preparation

What to Bring

Required Documents

  • A copy of your criminal record
  • A valid photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport)
How to get your criminal record: Hamilton County Sheriff's Office
1000 Sycamore Street, Cincinnati OH 45202
Cost: $5.00 cash only — bring your ID

Tip: Get your record a few days before your appointment so you have time to review it.

Day-of Tips

  • Arrive 5–10 minutes early for your scheduled appointment
  • Come alone if possible — space is limited
  • Bring any court documents related to your case
  • Questions before the clinic? Email RC@commafoundation.com
Filing fees are covered. The Comma Foundation pays all court filing fees on your behalf upon confirmation of your appointment. You pay nothing on clinic day.
